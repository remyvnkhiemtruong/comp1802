Our story / HIEN

A quiet porch is waiting.

A bright room, a comfortable chair, and a stay with time to spare.

Why we made HIEN?

We wanted a clearer way to explore homestays. This website lets you browse rooms, compare options, and try the booking workflow in your browser.

How to use this site

Use the main listing to search, filter, and open details. You can also create a demo account, save feedback, or add your own content.

This is a course project. Orders, appointments, and contact forms are demonstrations; no payment is processed or information sent to an outside business.
